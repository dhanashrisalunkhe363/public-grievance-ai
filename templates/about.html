Our Mission

Our mission is to make public grievance management more organized, transparent and accessible. The system combines Natural Language Processing and an LSTM Deep Learning model to analyze complaint text and provide useful information such as sentiment, category, priority and prediction confidence. This helps administrators understand complaints and manage them more efficiently.

PLATFORM CAPABILITIES

Built for citizens and administrators

The platform provides separate features for citizens and administrators while using AI to support complaint analysis.

01 / CITIZEN

Citizen Friendly

Citizens can register, login, submit public complaints, view AI analysis results and track the progress of their complaints through their dashboard.

02 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI-Powered Analysis

NLP preprocessing and an LSTM Deep Learning model analyze complaint text, classify sentiment and provide a model confidence score.

03 / ADMINISTRATION

Admin Management

Administrators can review complaints, identify high-priority issues, update complaint status and monitor grievance-related information.

WORKFLOW

How Public Grievance AI Works

The system follows a simple workflow from complaint registration to resolution and notification.

01

Register & Login

Users create an account and securely access the public grievance portal.

02

Submit Complaint

The citizen describes the public issue and submits the relevant complaint details.

03

AI Analysis

NLP preprocessing prepares the complaint text and the LSTM model predicts its sentiment.

04

Category & Priority

The system identifies the complaint category and determines an appropriate priority level.

05

Admin Review & Resolution

Administrators review the complaint, update its status and add a solution or remark.

06

Notify & Track

Users can track complaint progress and receive email notifications for important updates.
